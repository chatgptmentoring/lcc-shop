Customizable Contract Template

To effectively prevent misunderstandings and scope creep, use this customizable contract template tailored for wedding planners. Fill in the sections with your specific details to create a clear agreement with your clients.

---

Contract Title: Wedding Planning Services Agreement

Date: [Insert Date]

Parties Involved:

  • Planner: [Your Name/Company Name]
  • Client: [Client's Name]

1. Scope of Work

Clearly outline the services you will provide. Include details such as:

  • Venue selection
  • Vendor coordination
  • Timeline management
  • Budget management

Example:

*The Planner agrees to provide the following services: venue selection, coordination with vendors, and management of the wedding timeline and budget. Any additional services requested by the Client will be billed separately.*

2. Client Responsibilities

Define what you expect from the client to ensure smooth collaboration.

Example:

*The Client agrees to provide necessary information, access to venues, and timely responses to the Planner's inquiries.*

3. Payment Terms

Specify the total cost, payment schedule, and accepted payment methods.

Example:

*The total fee for services is [Insert Amount]. A deposit of [Insert Amount] is due upon signing this agreement, with the remaining balance due [Insert Due Date].*

4. Change Order Process

Outline how changes to the scope will be handled.

Example:

*Any changes to the original scope of work must be documented in writing and may incur additional fees. The Planner will provide a change order form for the Client to approve.*

5. Cancellation Policy

Include your cancellation policy to protect your time and resources.

Example:

*If the Client cancels the agreement within [Insert Timeframe], the deposit will be non-refundable. Any payments made beyond the deposit will be refunded according to the services rendered at the time of cancellation.*

6. Signatures

Both parties should sign to acknowledge agreement to the terms.

Example:

*By signing below, both parties agree to the terms outlined in this contract.*

Planner's Signature: _________________________

Client's Signature: _________________________

---

Make sure to adjust any section according to your specific needs and the details of the wedding you are planning. This template will help you maintain clear boundaries and ensure you are fairly compensated for your work.

Amendment Clauses

In the event that changes or additional requests arise during the planning process, the following amendment clauses will apply to ensure that both parties are clear on expectations and compensation.

  1. Request for Changes: Any changes to the original scope of work must be submitted in writing by the client. This includes, but is not limited to, alterations in the event date, venue, guest count, or specific services requested.
  1. Scope of Work Review: Upon receipt of the change request, the planner will review the implications of the requested changes on the overall project. This review will include an assessment of how the changes affect timelines, budgets, and vendor coordination.
  1. Compensation for Additional Work: If the requested changes require additional work beyond the original agreement, the planner will provide a revised estimate of costs. This estimate will include any additional hours worked, vendor fees, or other expenses incurred due to the changes.
  1. Approval of Amendments: The client must approve any revised estimates in writing before the planner proceeds with the requested changes. No additional work will be undertaken until both parties have agreed on the new terms.
  1. Documentation of Amendments: All agreed-upon changes will be documented in an amendment to this contract. This amendment will outline the specific changes, the new compensation structure, and any adjusted timelines.
  1. Final Review: Both parties will conduct a final review of the amended contract to ensure understanding and agreement on the changes. This will serve to protect both the planner's boundaries and the client's interests.

By adhering to these amendment clauses, both the wedding planner and client can navigate the complexities of event planning while ensuring that all changes are compensated fairly and documented clearly.
